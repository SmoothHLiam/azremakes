HOW TO PLAY
▲◀▼▶

↑ / W / Space  jump over low obstacles

↓ / S  dive under the water (and under hanging logs and pipes)

← → / A D  steer

Esc / P  pause

On touch screens: drag sideways to steer, swipe up to jump, swipe down to dive.

Dive straight into a turtle and it will fling you high into the air!

Your board can take one knock on each side. Clip something and you get pushed the other way and that side cracks. Hit a cracked side again, or smash into something head-on, and the run is over.

You can land on top of boats, docks and fallen slabs.

Wrench repairs one cracked side of your board (left side first).

Freeze slows you down by 25% for 15 seconds.

Shield absorbs one collision within 15 seconds.

Magnet pulls nearby seashells to you for 15 seconds.

Star doubles all points for 15 seconds.

Powerups show up less often in Hard Mode.

A fan-made remake of the browser surfing game Wave Rider. All code, art and sound here are original and made procedurally in the browser.

Spend seashells in the shop to unlock new boards, finish daily missions and achievements for extra shells, and try Hard Mode once you get the hang of it.
